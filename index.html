Meet the owner

I started with one glass counter and my father on a plastic stool.

In 2011 I opened this shop in Kendrapara Town. On the first day my father sat beside me for hours and gave me one rule: never sell a customer a phone you would not buy for your own family. I have kept to it for fifteen years.

The first phone I sold was a keypad set to a farmer from a village near Kendrapara. The next week he came back with his son to buy a charger and told me the phone had not stopped ringing. I still remember that more than any big sale.

Phones have gone from keypad to 5G, but people still come the same way: with their parents, their children, their old phone and their doubts. I answer every doubt, even when it means telling you to buy the cheaper phone.

  • Straight advice. I tell you which phone not to buy, not only which one to buy.
  • Price told upfront, in rupees, with no add-on surprises at the counter.
  • Sealed-box phones with a proper bill and brand warranty.
  • Contacts, photos and WhatsApp moved to your new phone before you leave.
  • Call or WhatsApp at any hour. The shop runs 24×7 because a dead phone cannot wait for morning.
